# Which motor is right for me?

**Reading time:** 8 min · **Difficulty:** Easy

Use this guide to pick the right motor series for your load, duty cycle, and power supply.

---

## Quick comparison

| | BLDC (AL57 / AL86) | Induction (IEC 63–132) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Supply | 24 V – 48 V DC | 3-phase / single-phase AC | — |
| Speed range | 0 – 4000 RPM | 750 – 3000 RPM | BLDC is continuously variable |
| Efficiency | ★★★★★ | ★★★ | BLDC wins at partial load |
| Maintenance | Very low (brushless) | Low (no brushes) | — |
| Cost | Higher upfront | Lower upfront | — |
| Best for | Precise speed control, battery systems | Fixed-speed industrial loads | — |

---

## Choose BLDC if…

- You need precise speed control or positioning
- Your supply is DC (battery, solar, 24/48 V bus)
- Efficiency at partial load matters (conveyors, pumps running at 30–70% speed)
- You want a compact, lightweight motor

**Recommended series:** AL57 (up to 750 W) · AL86 (up to 3 kW)

---

## Choose Induction if…

- You have a standard 3-phase or single-phase AC supply
- The load runs at a fixed speed most of the time
- You want proven, lowest-cost reliability for high-duty applications
- Motor is running in a high-ambient-temperature environment

**Recommended series:** IEC 63 (0.18 kW) through IEC 132 (7.5 kW)
