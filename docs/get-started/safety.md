# Safety basics before you start

**Reading time:** 4 min · **Difficulty:** Easy

Read this before touching any wiring or mounting hardware.

---

## Required PPE

| Item | When required |
|------|--------------|
| Insulated gloves (1000 V rated) | Any wiring work |
| Safety glasses | Drilling, mounting, terminal work |
| Safety boots (steel toe) | Lifting motors > 5 kg |
| Hearing protection | Test runs in enclosed spaces |

---

## Lock-out tag-out (LOTO)

Always LOTO before working on a wired motor:

1. Switch off the supply isolator
2. Lock the isolator in the OFF position with your personal padlock
3. Tag the isolator with your name and date
4. Wait 5 minutes for capacitors to discharge
5. Verify zero voltage with a multimeter between each phase and ground

:::danger
Never assume a motor is safe because it has stopped spinning. Residual voltage can remain for several minutes after power-off.
:::

---

## Grounding requirements

- The motor frame **must** be bonded to the PE (protective earth) terminal
- Earth conductor must be the same cross-section as the phase conductors
- Use a ring terminal crimped and torqued to **3 N·m** on the PE stud

---

## IP ratings — what they mean

| Rating | Dust | Water | Where to use |
|--------|------|-------|-------------|
| IP44 | Protected | Splashproof | Dry indoor |
| IP54 | Dustproof | Splashproof | General industrial |
| IP65 | Dusttight | Jet-proof | Washdown areas |

Alto BLDC motors are rated **IP54** as standard. IP65 is available on request.
